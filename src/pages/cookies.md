# Cookies Policy

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember your preferences and improve functionality.

## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Improve website performance
- Analyze traffic with Google Analytics
- Display personalized ads through Google AdSense
- Remember user preferences

## Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are provided by third-party services, including:

- Google Analytics
- Google AdSense

These services may collect anonymous usage information.

## Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Please note that some features of the website may not function properly if cookies are disabled.
