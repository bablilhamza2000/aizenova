Yes. Claude offers a free version, while paid plans provide additional capabilities and higher usage limits.

## Is Claude good for coding?

Yes. Claude performs very well for programming, debugging, explaining code, and software development tasks.

## Can Claude analyze PDFs?

Yes. Claude can analyze uploaded documents, summarize them, extract key information, and answer questions based on their contents.

## Is Claude better than ChatGPT?

It depends on your needs. Claude is particularly strong at handling long documents and producing polished writing, while ChatGPT offers a broader feature set that may include image generation, advanced productivity tools, and other capabilities depending on the plan.

## Is Claude worth paying for?

If you regularly work with long documents, technical writing, research, or software development, a paid Claude subscription can provide significant productivity benefits.
