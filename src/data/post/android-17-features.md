# What Is Android 17?

Android 17 is the latest major version of Google's mobile operating system.

Rather than completely redesigning Android, Google focused on improving the features people use every day.

The update introduces smarter multitasking, improved security, more powerful creator tools, and better performance while preparing Android for an increasingly AI-driven future. Google also describes Android 17 as the beginning of a transition toward a more intelligence-focused platform for apps and devices. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Unlike previous releases that emphasized visual changes, Android 17 concentrates on making your phone more useful without changing the familiar Android experience.
